nodes are still usable via the text-input pair list in
src/components/workerempire/WorkerEmpireView.tsx; they just don't have a
clickable marker on the map canvas. See
docs/handoff-worker-empire-optimizer-2026-09-05.md for the coordinate
calibration this was verified against.
