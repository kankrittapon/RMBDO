  5. 🏆 `TREASURE FARM` (Pure Movement Speed + Giant AoE One-Shot setup)
- **Sub-components**: Recommended Crystals, Artifacts (Kabua/Monster), Lightstone Sets (Deathblow / Target Opening), Skill Add-on Presets.
