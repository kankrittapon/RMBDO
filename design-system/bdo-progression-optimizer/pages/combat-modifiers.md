## 🗡️ Back Attack Modifier Rules & Build Integration

1. **Back Attack Multiplier System**:
   - **Base Back Attack Damage**: $1.5\times$ (150% Base Multiplier).
   - **Special Attack Damage Scaling**:
     - Corrupted Magic Crystal x2: $+20\%$ Critical Damage.
     - Ah'Krad Crystal x2: $+14$ Monster AP / Attack Speed.
     - Decisive / Back Attack Artifacts + Lightstone Combos (e.g. *Target Opening / Wild Back Attack*): $+15\%$ to $+22\%$ Additional Back Attack Damage.
     - Skill Add-on: $+3\%$ to $+5\%$ Back Attack Damage on CC/Initiator skills.

2. **When to Prioritize Back Attack Builds**:
   - **High-End Dehkia Spots & Olun's Valley**: Monsters have massive HP pools and directional vulnerability (Positioning behind mob yields up to $+35\%$ total DPS increase).
   - **Boss Blitz / Morning Light Bosses**: Bosses have fixed turn rates; flanking enables maximum burst rotations.

3. **UI Integration in Build Optimizer**:
   - Tag: `[FOCUS: BACK ATTACK + CRITICAL BURST]`
   - Visual Position Indicator: Back attack uptime estimation gauge ($70\% - 95\%$).
