## 🚦 Phase Status States

1. `Completed` (Emerald Green `#10B981` | Glow border, Checkmark icon)
2. `In Progress` (Arcane Purple `#8B5CF6` | Pulsing indicator, Progress ring)
3. `Blocked` (Crimson Red `#EF4444` | Warning shield, Blocker callout)
4. `Available` (Bright Blue `#3B82F6` | Ready to start, High ROI tag)
5. `Future` (Slate Gray `#64748B` | Locked/Prerequisite needed)

## 📑 Phase Detail Drawer / Modal Structure (On Click)

- **Header**: Phase Name + Status Badge + GS Bracket Target
- **Requirements**: AP/DP Minimums, Quest Pre-requisites, Level Requirements
- **Rewards**: Stat increases (AP/DP/HP), Unlocked Grind Spots, System Unlocks
- **Items Required**: Core materials (e.g. Flame of Primordial, Caphras, Boss Cores)
- **Items to Preserve**: ⚠️ Important items NOT to sell or overwrite
- **Estimated Cost**: Total Silver, Cron Stones needed, Time estimate (Hours)
- **Optimal Grind Locations**: Best Silver/Hr and Material Spots for this stage
- **Exit Conditions / Milestone Checklist**: Exact criteria to transition to the next phase

## ⚡ Task Priority Grouping

- **[HIGH PRIORITY]**: Immediate ROI, AP/DP Bracket jump, Free permanent stats
- **[THIS WEEK]**: Weekly Dungeons (Atoraxxion), Guild Bosses, Weekly Season Quests
- **[LATER]**: Long-term grind (Deboreka PEN, Infinite Compass, Merchant Ring)
