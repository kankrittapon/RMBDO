## 🔨 Enhancement Tiers & Protection Meta (2026 Live Meta)

1. **Sovereign Weapons (PRI -> DEC / +10)**:
   - Uses **Primordial Black Stones** & **Dawn Essences**.
   - No equipment degradation up to +5 (TRI); protected failure states.
   - Recommended Failstack Matrix:
     - +1 to +5 (PRI -> TRI): 100 - 150 FS
     - +6 to +8 (TET -> OCT): 200 - 280 FS + Cron Stones
     - +9 to +10 (NOV -> DEC): 300+ FS + J's Hammers of Precision

2. **Slumbering Origin Armors (Base -> OBLIVION / +5)**:
   - Base -> DUO: 100 - 140 FS (No crons needed for PRI/DUO).
   - TRI (Desolate): 160 - 200 FS + Cron Stones (Cost: 2,100 Crons / try).
   - TET (Silent): 240 - 280 FS + Cron Stones (Cost: 4,200 Crons / try).
   - PEN (Oblivion): 300+ FS + J's Hammer of Antiquity.

3. **Kharazad Accessories**:
   - Upgraded with Dawn Essences / Crystal sockets.
   - Zero item explosion risk upon failure; utilizes safe recovery catalysts.
