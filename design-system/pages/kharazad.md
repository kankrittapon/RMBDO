## 💎 Kharazad Accessory System Overview

1. **Crafting & Integration**:
   - Upgrade from existing TET/PEN Deboreka / Tungrad / Disto.
   - Dedicated **Essence / Crystal Slot** inside the accessory itself.
   - Eliminates accessory destruction risk upon enhancement failure (downgrades or uses specific essence protection).

2. **Progression Priority & Efficiency**:
   - **Necklace**: Highest AP increase per silver tier.
   - **Belt**: AP + Weight + Special evasion/monster DR.
   - **Rings**: AP + Monster AP bracket booster.
   - **Earrings**: AP + Accuracy scaling.

3. **Blockers & Resource Tracking**:
   - Dawn / Primordial Essences.
   - Land of the Morning Light 2 / Seoul Boss Blitz drops.
   - Silver requirement vs. PEN Deboreka comparison.
